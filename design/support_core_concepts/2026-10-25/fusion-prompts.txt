## F1
Design intent: invent ONE brand-new unified form that fuses two ideas into a single continuous sculpted mass: (1) a tall tapering rune-carved stone obelisk crowned by a floating teal-cyan crystal, and (2) four cross-shaped conduit arms that carry energy outward to the four tile edges. Do NOT draw an obelisk placed on top of a separate cross base, and do NOT bolt arms or sockets onto an existing tower. There must be no visible seam between "tower" and "arms": the arms are the tower itself. The silhouette must be clearly new, not a previous concept with parts added.
This variant, "rooted spire": the obelisk flares at its foot into four thick buttress roots, one per cardinal direction, that sweep down in a smooth continuous curve and run flat along the ground to the tile edge, like the spire growing out of the ground along four ridges. Seen from above the whole structure is one four-pointed star tapering up into a point. The four cyan rune channels start right under the floating crystal, run down the four spire ridges and continue unbroken along each root to a small glowing cut facet at the root tip. Antique-bronze bands wrap the spire and the roots as one shared band pattern.
